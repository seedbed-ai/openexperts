# Approval evidence

## Approval evidence

A person who likes a proposal is not necessarily the person who may approve the expenditure. For a fictional opportunity, record separately: the proposed user, the decision participant, the holder of expenditure authority, and the source that confirms each role.

If no reliable statement identifies the approver, record **economic buyer: unknown** and **budget authority: unverified**. A sales contact's interest cannot substitute for authorization.

Suggested bounded inquiry: ask the current contact which role approves the budget and what approval step follows the funding review. Record answers as attributed statements until independently confirmed.
