# Evidence-based next action

## Inputs

A customer-supplied situation, source and date of each material fact, current authorization to communicate, and known/unknown buyer approval and timing information.

## Procedure

1. Extract observed facts without inventing titles, commitments or financial metrics.
2. Mark unsupported buyer identity, budget authority and purchasing date as unknown.
3. Select the relevant [approval evidence](APPROVAL.md#approval-evidence) or [decision timing](DELAY.md#decision-timing) section.
4. Recommend the smallest useful question to resolve the next dependency. For an unconfirmed funding review, seek the authorized approval contact and a follow-up point, not a fabricated close date.
5. If a message is helpful, draft it for human review. Do not send, log a CRM update or modify task state without current permission.
6. Report consulted source paths, pinned revision, remaining unknowns and the proposed next action.

## Fictional illustration

A prospect says it will revisit the purchase after its next funding review. The assistant records **funding unconfirmed**, **economic buyer unidentified**, and **no committed purchase date**. Proposed next step: ask the contact who can authorize expenditure and when funding status can be checked. It does not characterize the deal as closed or funded.
