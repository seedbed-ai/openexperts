# Decision timing

## Decision timing

A decision deferred until a future funding review has no confirmed purchase date unless the prospect supplies one. Separate the date of a review from the date of actual spending approval and from a signed commitment.

Do not invent a cost of delay, competitor, internal deadline or budget. If appropriate, ask whether the prospect expects a concrete decision after the review and when a factual update may be available. Preserve any uncertainty in the task record.
