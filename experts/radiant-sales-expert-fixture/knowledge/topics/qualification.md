## Use and maintenance

An index entry does not substantiate a deal-specific assertion. Read the selected source section, then the **selected function/process section** if an actual professional recommendation is required. Missing customer facts require a question or qualified conclusion. The MEDDPICC document is a methodology fixture, not authoritative validation of customer records.

**Provenance:** linked document paths are relative to this package; observed `expert.yaml.version` and repository commit identify a reading. Source authorship, source freshness and truth require independent evidence. On maintenance, review anchor `#criteria` and `#usage`, capability cross-links and applicability limits.
